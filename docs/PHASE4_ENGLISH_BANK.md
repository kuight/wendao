## Q01–Q10
1. Choose the synonym of **arduous**. A.difficult B.swift C.plain D.silent · Answer: A
2. Choose the antonym of **ephemeral**. A.transient B.perpetual C.sudden D.fragile · Answer: B
3. Fill in the blank: The cultivator remained ___ in practice despite setbacks. A.assiduous B.cursory C.idle D.brittle · Answer: A
4. Choose the correct adverb: He explained the formula ___. A.clear B.clearly C.clearness D.clearest · Answer: B
5. Identify the figure of speech: “Moonlight stitched silver seams across the lake.” A.simile B.metaphor C.irony D.hyperbole · Answer: B
6. Choose the correct participle: The talisman had been ___ before dawn. A.carved B.carve C.carving D.carves · Answer: A
7. Passive voice: The senior sister brewed the tea. · Answer: The tea was brewed by the senior sister.
8. 汉译英：那位老修士在炼丹炉旁守了整整三天三夜。· Answer: The old cultivator kept watch beside the alchemy furnace for three days and nights.
9. If I ___ a spirit sword, I would roam the nine heavens. A.had B.have C.would have D.am having · Answer: A
10. Choose the article: She inherited ___ innate talent. A.a B.an C.the D./ · Answer: B

## Q11–Q20
11. What is the comparative form of **far**? A.farther B.further C.both A and B D.farrer · Answer: C
12. Reading: “Even the wind paused to listen.” Device? A.onomatopoeia B.personification C.alliteration D.assonance · Answer: B
13. 汉译英：学问如炼丹，急不得。· Answer: Learning is like refining an elixir; it cannot be rushed.
14. Choose the preposition: The scroll is bound ___ scarlet silk. A.with B.by C.in D.of · Answer: C
15. Identify the error: Neither the master nor his disciples were prepared. A.master B.disciples C.were D.prepared · Answer: C
16. Choose the closest meaning of **luminous**. A.dim B.radiant C.opaque D.muted · Answer: B
17. Reported speech: The elder said, “A storm will rise tonight.” · Answer: The elder said that a storm would rise that night.
18. 汉译英：即使是最冷的长夜，炉火也不会熄灭。· Answer: Even on the coldest night, the furnace fire will not go out.
19. Practise daily, ___ you shall improve. A.lest B.or C.and D.yet · Answer: C
20. Pick the correct spelling. A.accomodate B.accommodate C.acomodate D.accommadate · Answer: B

## Q21–Q30
21. Clause type: “What the jade tablet foretold” is a ___. A.subject noun clause B.object noun clause C.adverbial clause D.relative clause · Answer: A
22. Write one sentence (≤20 words) describing the cave alchemy scene. · Sample: Moonlight slipped into the cave while the furnace hummed softly.
23. Choose the best idiom: He finally finished the manuscript; he ___. A.bit the bullet B.saw it through C.crossed the Rubicon D.saw the light · Answer: B
24. 汉译英：我宁愿在山间小道独行，也不愿在繁华城中迷失。· Answer: I would rather walk alone on the mountain path than lose myself in a bustling city.
25. Hardly ___ the incense burned out when the bell rang. A.had B.did C.have D.was · Answer: A
26. Choose the best verb: The cultivator ___ the spirit sword with grace. A.wielded B.holded C.grasped D.carried · Answer: A
27. Which sentence is correct? A.Each of the disciples have their own spirit sword. B.Each of the disciples has their own spirit sword. C.Each disciples has their own spirit sword. D.Each of the disciple have a spirit sword. · Answer: B
28. “Fame may rise like smoke, but merit settles like stone.” Device? A.antithesis B.oxymoron C.anaphora D.epistrophe · Answer: A
29. Compose one sentence with a metaphor about practice. · Sample: Practice is a quiet forge where patience tempers talent.
30. Pick the antonym of **magnanimous**. A.generous B.petty C.brave D.clever · Answer: B
